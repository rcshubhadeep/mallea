# Concept catalogue content

E003's clearly illustrative app catalogue. Six bundled original SVG illustrations and preview descriptions are implemented in the dashboard, not installed third-party apps. Entries are labelled Preview concept; there are no downloads, ratings, users or working Install. No scraped brand assets or remote image dependencies.

| Name | Purpose | Cover direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lumen | A writing desk whose notes grow into connected ideas | Warm cream/amber, layered paper and a small light motif |
| Daylight | A flexible planning desk for dates and available slots | Pale sky blue, sun arc and modular calendar tiles |
| Threadboard | Review feedback becomes an actionable workspace | Plum/coral, connected cards and a simple thread path |
| Folio | A personal collection of references and project material | Deep green, overlapping image frames and index tabs |
| Relay | A workspace for explicitly permitted app-to-app data | Midnight blue, two illuminated panels with a data path |
| Stillroom | Focus sessions with disposable working Surfaces | Soft lavender, quiet concentric forms and a timer motif |

Fieldnote and Parcel are real demo apps and may appear separately as included examples once their native builds work. Keep their actual author/purpose/capabilities tied to registered metadata. Concept cards can open a read-only preview description; they must not pretend to launch working apps.
